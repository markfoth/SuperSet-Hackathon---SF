## Correctness and remaining limits

Required home-to-destination gates remain hard. Pending, failed or stale routing cannot confirm matches. Missing measurements stay unknown and retain source geography. Environmental context and demographic proxies do not enter ranking. Older order-independent wording means input-edit history, while explicit priority order deliberately changes lexicographic bands; unit and browser regressions cover both.

Terrain is external best-effort context with mixed source dates/resolution, not a survey or photorealistic mesh. Nine mapped park entrances are sparse and not verified accessible routes. Proximity is geometric; no barrier-aware walking network was established. Measured fairness requires configured evaluation routing and at least two eligible homes with complete current journeys. Provider rights, source gaps and inherited CalEnviroScreen redistribution uncertainty remain documented. No paid API, listing scrape, household backend or production infrastructure change was added.
